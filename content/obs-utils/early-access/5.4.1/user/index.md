OBS Utils Premium 5.4.1 gives your Foundry stream its own camera, overlays and OBS controls. This early-access edition adds a dedicated Setup window, camera animation editing, scene data for overlays and experimental 3D Canvas camera controls.

Start with [Getting started](./getting-started/), then use the [Stream Composer](./stream-composer/) to build your overlays.

## Guides

- [Setup and importing settings](./setup/)
- [Camera modes](./camera-modes/)
- [Camera presets and animations](./scene-presets/)
- [Manual OBS mode](./manual-mode/)
- [OBS Remote](./obs-remote/)
- [Overlay animations](./overlay-animations/)

D&D 5e, Pathfinder 2e and Call of Cthulhu integrations are separate companion modules. Enable the companion for your game system to add its actor fields, events and starter overlays.

This release includes English and German. Other interface languages fall back to English.

For help, join [Discord](https://discord.com/invite/WfMaKPPdeM). [Developer docs](../dev/) cover adding events, actor values and Director tabs.

## 3D Canvas credit

This module features the [3D Canvas](https://theripper93.com/module/levels-3d-preview) module by [theripper93](https://theripper93.com/) through its optional camera integration.
