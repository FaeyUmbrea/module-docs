---
title: "Camera presets and animations"
---

Open the Director's **Presets** tab to save views for the current scene.

**Add Static** saves one camera position. Give it a name you'll recognise during the session, then apply it when you want the stream to return there. Update it after moving to a better view.

**Add Animation** opens the keyframe editor. Keyframes describe where the camera should be at each point in time, including its zoom. Use them for a slow reveal, a pan across a scene, or a route the audience should follow.

Preview the timing before using it live. Animation presets play on the OBS client so you can keep working in your own GM view. Use **Stop** in the Presets tab to halt playback.

Presets belong to their scene. Switching scenes changes the list, so give each scene the views it needs.
