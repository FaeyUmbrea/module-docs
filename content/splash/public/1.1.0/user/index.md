title: "Splash: Interactive Handouts"
---

Splash turns journal pages into interactive handouts: fullscreen screens, clickable puzzles, buttons, and objects you can arrange in an editor. You build the handout, then decide when your players see it.

This is the guide for **1.1.0**. The release requires Foundry **13.344** or newer within its supported range.

[Get started](./getting-started/) with installation and setup.
